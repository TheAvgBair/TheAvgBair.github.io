Portfolio site privacy

Optional means optional.

The portfolio can offer anonymous, first-party measurement of outbound product-link clicks. Measurement remains off unless you choose “Allow.”

What a consented click can contain

The product ID, campaign month, Reel occurrence or hub content ID when available, destination hostname, and observation time. It does not include message content, an advertising profile, or a claimed downstream install or purchase.

Unavailable results

When an App Store install, marketplace purchase, or other downstream conversion cannot be observed, TheAvgDevs records it as unavailable—not zero and not an estimate.

Your choice

Your browser stores only the portfolio measurement choice. You can clear this site’s local storage to reset it. Product destinations have their own privacy policies and choices.
