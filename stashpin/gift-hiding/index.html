Hide the gift. Keep the clue.

A “perfect hiding place” can work too well. StashPin's Gift Hiding Mode saves the gift and hidden location with the details that will bring the memory back at the right time.

A short form for a very specific job.

Gift Hiding Mode keeps the useful details together as one private item.

  • Gift: What you hid
  • Hidden location: The route back to it
  • Recipient and occasion: The context you may search later
  • Notes: Receipt location, missing accessory, or another helpful detail
  • Status: Hidden, wrapped, or given
  • Optional reminder: A date you choose in the app

Private means out of the ordinary list when you choose.

Gift Hiding Mode saves the record as a private, important item.

StashPin includes a “Hide Private Items” setting. When it is on, private records are filtered from the regular item, location, tag, search, and recently deleted views. Turn the setting off when you need to work with them.

The private-item setting is a visibility control inside the app, not a substitute for your iPhone passcode or other device security.

Make the hidden location useful to future you.

“Closet” may feel obvious today. A little more structure helps weeks later.

Start broad, then narrow: “Guest room closet → top shelf → behind the blue suitcase.” If the receipt or a small accessory lives somewhere else, put that in the notes instead of trusting it to memory.

  • Use a location that does not reveal the gift in its name
  • Add the recipient as entered context so you can search in the words you remember
  • Choose the optional reminder only when a date will be useful
  • Update or delete the record after the gift is given
