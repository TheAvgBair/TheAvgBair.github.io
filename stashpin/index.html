Good to know.

Where does StashPin keep my information?

Your items, locations, notes, tags, selected photos, settings, and backups are stored locally on your device.

Do I need an account?

No. StashPin does not require an account and does not use in-app advertising, analytics, or tracking.

Can I try it before unlocking unlimited items?

Yes. You can keep up to 10 active items for free. Unlimited items are available through an optional subscription in the app.
