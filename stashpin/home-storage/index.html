Organize home storage without memorizing every shelf.

Closets, cabinets, bins, and drawers make a home easier to live in—until you cannot remember which one holds the thing you need. StashPin lets you save a simple, searchable clue.

Save the clue, not an entire inventory.

You do not need to list everything you own. Record the things that would otherwise send you opening boxes and checking shelves.

Write locations the way you would say them.

A useful storage clue sounds natural, not like a warehouse code.

Start with the room or area, then add the container and the small detail that separates it from nearby spots. “Office closet, blue bin, back left” is easier to recognize than a label you would never use in conversation.

  • Name the object clearly. Add an alternate name when someone in your home might call it something else.
  • Use the location field for the route. Room, cabinet, bin, shelf, or drawer can all be part of the clue.
  • Add a note for the awkward detail. “Behind the photo albums” may be the part that actually gets you there.
  • Choose a photo when words are not enough. A picture can distinguish similar bins, shelves, or boxes.

A simple routine that fits real life.

Search in your own words

Look by item, location, tag, note, or alternate name when you need it.

StashPin is especially useful for the things you need occasionally: important papers, spare parts, seasonal pieces, backup supplies, and the “safe place” you will otherwise forget.
