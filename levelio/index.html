Privacy is part of the product

Your tasks are not a profile.

Levelio does not require an account. Your tasks, habits, character progress, and settings stay on your device unless you choose an optional Apple service or share an export or diagnostic report yourself.

Private iCloud backup is optional. Optional rewarded ads for free users are limited to user-chosen battle-assist actions; your task and habit data are not sent to ad providers for targeting.

FAQ

Good to know before you start.

Is Levelio a game or a task app?

It is a task and habit tracker with an RPG reward layer. Your real completion is what creates the progress.

Do I need an account?

No. Levelio can be used without an account or login.

What can I do for free?

The free tier includes core task and habit tracking, basic reminders, RPG progress, and local backup controls. Levelio Pro adds unlimited active tracking and advanced companion, history, and backup options.

Can I keep a backup?

Yes. Local backup controls are available in the app. Levelio Pro also includes optional private iCloud backup and sync.
